# ZYREX DRIVE — PHASE 16

## Economy & Progression
- Local wallet starts at Rp25.000.
- BMW M3 E30 Stance is the starter vehicle and remains owned.
- Other vehicles have local purchase prices and start locked.
- Race placement rewards cash: 1st Rp5.000, 2nd Rp3.000, 3rd Rp2.000, 4th Rp1.000, 5th Rp750.
- Garage modifications have configurable local costs.
- Vehicle repair costs Rp20 per damage percentage point.
- Ownership, cash, earned/spent totals are saved in localStorage.
- Profile schema is version 3 and carries economy data.
- No new backend/database is introduced.
